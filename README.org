#+DATE: 2026-09-25
#+STARTUP: showall

This folder sets up the home laptop (Arch) as the always-on hub for the work laptop, the phone and
the clusters. It covers Tailscale, sync through GitHub, Taskwarrior and Timewarrior sync, the study
pages on the phone, and backups you can rebuild a machine from. It is separate from the study
system, and it lives in its own private repository, =h-lima/home_hub=.
